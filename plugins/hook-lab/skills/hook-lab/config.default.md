## Content Pillars
- Marketing & Consumer Behavior (~50%) — why people buy, how messages spread, what drives decisions
- AI & Productivity (~30%) — AI tools applied to real workflows, practical adoption
- Career & Industry Insights (~20%) — business stories, professional development, industry patterns

## Voice
Data-driven professional who makes complex ideas feel simple and actionable. Analytical but approachable — shows their work, cites specifics, never preaches.

## Audience
Marketing and business professionals on LinkedIn (mid-career, 5–15 years experience). They follow thought leaders but have a finely tuned BS detector. They stop for surprising data and real stories. They skip recycled advice and motivational fluff.

## Personas (defaults — replace via setup)

### Persona 1: The Skeptical Senior Marketer
- **Who**: Maya, 32. Senior Marketing Manager at a mid-size SaaS company. 8 years in marketing. Has seen every growth hack, every "revolutionary framework," every guru. Filters HARD.
- **What makes her stop**: Surprising data, genuine contrarian takes, specificity that proves the author actually knows what they're talking about.
- **Platform behavior**: Checks feed 2x/day, 10 min each. Scrolls fast. Clicks 2-3 posts per session. Never shares fluff.
- **Key question**: "Is this actually saying something, or just performing intelligence?"

### Persona 2: The Curious Career Switcher
- **Who**: Raj, 28. Former teacher, 1 year into a marketing coordinator role. Actively learning. Follows thought leaders, saves posts, takes notes.
- **What makes him stop**: Clear explanations of concepts he's heard but doesn't fully understand, practical how-tos, accessible takes on complex ideas.
- **Platform behavior**: Checks feed 3-4x/day, bookmarks heavily. Shares content that makes him look like he's growing professionally.
- **Key question**: "Will this teach me something I can use Monday morning?"

### Persona 3: The Time-Constrained VP
- **Who**: Diane, 41. VP of Marketing at a healthcare company. 200+ posts in her feed daily. Gives each hook 0.8 seconds before deciding to stop or scroll.
- **What makes her stop**: Bold claims she agrees or disagrees with, numbers that surprise her, anything that feels like insider knowledge.
- **Platform behavior**: Scrolls during meetings, in line for coffee, between calls. ~15 min total/day across micro-sessions. Clicks rarely — the hook must do ALL the work.
- **Key question**: "Why should I stop for this when I have 200 other posts competing for my attention?"

### Persona 4: The Data-Driven Analyst
- **Who**: Alex, 35. Marketing Analytics Lead at a fintech company. Lives in dashboards. Viscerally allergic to vague claims.
- **What makes them stop**: Data-driven insights, surprising statistics, posts that show their work. Specific numbers, named companies, cited sources.
- **Platform behavior**: Checks feed 1-2x/day. Reads fewer posts but reads them deeply. Comments with counterpoints. Shares things with data.
- **Key question**: "Where's the proof? Show me the number."

### Persona 5: The Professional Content Creator
- **Who**: Priya, 30. Full-time content strategist who also posts 3x/week on LinkedIn. Evaluates hooks professionally — she reverse-engineers every post.
- **What makes her stop**: Hooks she wishes she'd written, unusual structures, formats she hasn't seen before.
- **Platform behavior**: Studies the feed like a textbook. Saves hooks in a swipe file. Notices patterns. Shares content that's genuinely craft-worthy.
- **Key question**: "Would I save this hook to my swipe file?"
